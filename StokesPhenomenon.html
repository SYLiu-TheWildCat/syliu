c -Filtrations on Local System L associated to solutions to Airy systems.

The Airy system has two eigen-solutions with asymptotics ± 1 . The red regions correspond to stalks L ≤ c , θ = 0 , the purple one corresponds to L ≤ c , θ having rank 1 , and the blue region corresponds to L ≤ c , θ = L θ .

The lines have slope −cot(θ). The single-hatched min-positive region is red except blue for π/2 < θ < 3π/2. The cross-hatched max-negative region is blue for 0 ≤ θ < π/2 and 3π/2 < θ ≤ 2π, and red for π/2 < θ < 3π/2. The region strictly between the two lines is cross-hatched in purple.
